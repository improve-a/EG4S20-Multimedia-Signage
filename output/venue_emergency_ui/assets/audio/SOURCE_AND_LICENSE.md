# Original audio

Both WAV files are generated solely by `scripts/package_media.py` using standard-library oscillators. Original procedural score: Cmaj9 / Am7 / Fmaj7 / Gadd9 pads with sparse bell notes. No recordings, external samples, copyrighted melodies or third-party audio are used. These original sound assets are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0.

80 seconds, PCM signed 16-bit, 48 kHz, mono. Gentle two-second entrance, three-second fade, exact final second silence. The DEMO cue track is a quiet presentation chime, not a real evacuation alarm. Audio playback is independent of image switching and starts once per boot; no image-triggered cue, synchronization or EOF loop is claimed.
